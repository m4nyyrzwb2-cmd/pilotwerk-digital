Questions

Good to know.

Do guests need an app?

No. Apple Wallet is already on every iPhone, Google Wallet on most Android phones. Guests scan a code or tap their phone, and that's it.

What about guests without a smartphone?

Keep your paper cards. Both work side by side.

What happens to stamps on paper cards?

Your team transfers them at the first scan. Nobody loses a stamp.

What does my team need?

One phone with a camera per shop is enough. You can add as many staff phones as you like, at no extra cost. The scanner runs in the browser.

Can we change the reward later?

Yes, anytime. For example a free smoothie after 5 bowls, or double stamps on certain days.

Who owns the guest data?

You do. Guests join voluntarily and can leave anytime. You can export your list whenever you want.

How do we cancel?

Any month, with one WhatsApp message. No contract, no fees.
